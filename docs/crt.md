# Candle Range Theory (CRT)

Candle Range Theory focuses on the structure and behavior of candle ranges to understand market context.

## Main Concepts
- Candle range analysis
- Manipulation patterns
- Expansion behavior
- Confirmation conditions

## Beginner-Friendly Explanation
CRT looks at how price behaves within and around candle ranges to help identify whether a move is likely to continue, fail, or reverse.

## Research Use
CRT can be used alongside SMC to create a richer market context for research and explanation.
