## 11. Calendar, Session, and Roll Completeness

Every requested trade date requires one authoritative, versioned calendar row,
exact GC split-session intervals, holiday/early-close state, runtime tzdata match,
and a source-to-row audit. The current calendar intake is useful raw evidence but

Only fully closed canonical sessions may enter. Open intervals must contain every
expected five-minute slot unless an accepted source-coverage record proves the
absence is outside the source's required interval. Unknown, ambiguous, invalid,
or unexplained missing coverage rejects the affected complete-session atomic group;

Roll selection remains non-price-adjusted and uses only prior completed-session
volume with the accepted three-session confirmation rule. No current-session or
future-session volume, filename preference, price continuity, manual front-month
choice, or outcome evidence may choose a contract. Contract regimes are monotonic.

## 12. Atomic Grain and Conservation

The smallest promotable unit is one same-effective candidate group plus every
source bar, detector identity, feature row, and complete label horizon required by
that group. Complete sessions and contract-roll groups are indivisible whenever
splitting would change eligibility or provenance.

Each raw row maps to zero or one canonical bar; each canonical bar maps to zero or
one partition; each accepted candidate maps to exactly one feature row and one
complete label or to one explicit exclusion reason. Duplicate underlying moments,
resampled views, alternate source files, or overlapping label groups cannot cross
partitions. All input/output/excluded counts and integer volumes must reconcile
exactly.

4. zero duplicate canonical moments and zero cross-partition source moments;

11. deterministic byte-identical manifests and ordered IDs on two clean runs;

An excluded bad group does not authorize keeping later evidence if chronological
cutoff or prefix invariance cannot be proved.

## 14. Minimum Evidence-Resolution Thresholds

Pre-OOS promotion additionally requires:

- at least `100` complete TRAIN sessions, `40` complete VALIDATION sessions, and
  `40` complete CALIBRATION sessions;
- at least four distinct deterministically selected contract months overall and
  at least two selected contracts in TRAIN, one in VALIDATION, and one in
  CALIBRATION;
- at least `150` accepted complete-label TRAIN candidates, `50` VALIDATION
  candidates, and `50` CALIBRATION candidates;
- both bullish and bearish directions represented by at least `30/10/10`
  candidates in TRAIN/VALIDATION/CALIBRATION respectively;
- both positive `TARGET_FIRST` and negative
  `INVALIDATION_FIRST|TIMEOUT` classes represented by at least `30/10/10`
  complete labels in those partitions respectively;
- zero use of incomplete, invalid, ambiguous, or outcome-collision labels.

These are adequacy gates, not trading claims. If a threshold fails, the run stops.
Dates, setup rules, labels, or thresholds are not tuned to rescue it; any broader
acquisition requires a later prospective proposal.

## 16. Purge, Embargo, and Leakage Controls

Every candidate whose source or label interval crosses a partition boundary is
purged as a whole. After each fitting boundary, at least `12` eligible five-minute
bars are embargoed; the longer excluded date gaps in Section 10 remain controlling.
Same-effective candidates and candidates with overlapping label horizons are kept
in one partition or purged together.

Preprocessing statistics fit on TRAIN only. Model family and fixed hyperparameters
may use VALIDATION only after TRAIN is frozen. Calibration chooses one threshold
from CALIBRATION only after model choice is frozen. No metric from a later partition
may change an earlier-stage transform, feature, label, date, candidate, or rule.
Random row splitting, shuffled cross-validation, and backfilled histories are
forbidden.
