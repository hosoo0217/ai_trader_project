# AI Coaching Module

The AI Coaching Module is the educational layer of the project.

## Purpose
The system should teach the user, not only generate signals.

## Planned Behavior
- Explain why a trade was taken
- Explain why a trade hit stop loss
- Explain why a trade hit take profit
- Identify mistakes such as early entry, weak confirmation, poor session choice, or poor risk sizing
- Give a short lesson after each trade
- Assign a trade grade: A+, A, B, C, or D

## Example Output Formats
- Winning trade explanation
- Losing trade explanation
- No-trade recommendation
- Daily lesson

## Beginner-Friendly Explanation
The coaching module helps the user understand their decisions, learn from outcomes, and improve over time.
