# Risk Management

Risk management is a core part of the project and will be built as an educational system.

## Planned Rules
- Entry validation
- Stop loss explanation
- Take profit logic
- Risk per trade
- Daily loss limit
- No-trade conditions

## Beginner-Friendly Explanation
Risk management helps the user avoid overtrading, protect capital, and understand why a trade should be skipped when conditions are not favorable.

## Research Use
These rules will support both backtesting and paper trading before any live trading is considered.
