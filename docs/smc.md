# Smart Money Concepts (SMC)

Smart Money Concepts are used to study how large market participants may influence price movement.

## Core Ideas
- Market structure
- Break of Structure (BOS)
- Change of Character (CHoCH)
- Liquidity sweeps
- Order blocks
- Fair Value Gaps (FVG)
- Premium and discount zones

## Beginner-Friendly Explanation
SMC helps the user think about price as a story of liquidity, imbalance, and intent rather than just a simple trend line.

## Research Use
In this project, SMC is treated as a research tool for understanding market context and improving decision quality.
